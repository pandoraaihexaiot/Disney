Top Fun On Board

The most exciting things — ranked! Grown-up bits are marked too.

01
🎆

Fireworks At Sea! Everyone

The Lion King: Celebration in the Sky — the only cruise ship in the world with real fireworks over the ocean. Happens one night — find out which night on Day 1 and grab a good deck spot early.

02
🎢

Ironcycle Roller Coaster Everyone

The longest roller coaster at sea — you steer your own speed on lap two! Must be 120–195 cm tall. Good news: it's open and running (measure at home so there are no surprises). Closed-toe shoes needed.

03
🦸

Kids' Club — Oceaneer Club Ages 3–10 Free

The secret best thing! Design your own coaster and ride it on a screen, train as a super-hero in the WEB Workshop, and zoom down a giant Slinky-dog slide. Open ~9am to midnight.

04
💦

Water Slides + Splash Zone Kids

Two twisty slides in Toy Story Place (the red one is faster!) plus a whole splashy playground. Sunscreen on before you go up.

05
🏎️

Pym Racers & Groot Galaxy Spin Kids

Two more Marvel Landing rides — drive tiny shrink-tech cars, then spin up high with Groot's bass-boosted beats.

06
👑

Meet Characters + Royal Gathering Everyone

Characters roam the whole ship (check the app map!). The Royal Gathering meets lots of Princesses in one go — free, but book it (opens 4 Aug).

07
🎭

Big Broadway Shows Everyone

Moana: Call of the Sea, the brand-new Remember, and Avengers Assemble! with a giant screen. Book seats on Day 1 — they fill fast.

08
🌈

Glowing Reef + Bubble Tea Everyone

Discovery Reef lights up like a glow-in-the-dark ocean at night — and it's the first Disney ship with bubble tea! 🧋

🚫

Leave At Home

Found at bag screening = confiscated, and your luggage gets delayed to late evening. Not worth it.

  • 🔥Anything that heats or plugs in big: irons & steamers (the ship's laundry rooms have free irons), kettles, coffee makers, rice cookers, hot plates, blenders, heating pads, electric blankets, fairy lights, candles & incense.
  • 🔌Extension cords, power strips, multi-plugs & surge protectors — all banned. Bring single travel adapters only.
  • 💨High-wattage hair dryers (230V+) — a newer Disney rule. Under-230V dryers are fine, and the cabin has one. Curling/flat irons and electric shavers are allowed.
  • 🛩️Drones, RC vehicles, hoverboards & e-scooters.
  • 🤳Selfie sticks/tripods over 18 in (45 cm) must stay in the stateroom — and with no ports on this cruise, that means the whole trip. Bring a short one or none.
  • 🥡Homemade, perishable or opened food — only factory-sealed, non-perishable snacks are allowed aboard.
  • 🍾Alcohol over the allowance, opened bottles, or any alcohol in checked bags — removed without compensation.
  • 🔪Weapons, sharp objects, illegal drugs (including cannabis/CBD products).
🇸🇬 Singapore border — even stricter than the ship
  • 🚭Vapes & e-cigarettes are illegal in Singapore — bringing one across the border is an offence with heavy fines, and enforcement is aggressive. Leave them at home in Malaysia, not just off the ship.
  • ⛔Zero-tolerance drug laws — Singapore's penalties are among the world's harshest. Double-check bags weren't used by anyone else.
  • 🍬Chewing gum import is restricted in Singapore — easiest to just not bring any.

Extra Treasures (Add-Ons)

Grown-up stuff. The fare covers ~90% already — meals, shows, fireworks, rides, kids' club, soft drinks. These cost extra (USD; confirm in the app).

  1. 1Gratuities — basically not optional From ~US$16 / guest / night (auto-charged at the end) + 18% on paid drinks & dining. Family of 3 × 4 nights ≈ US$190+ before drinks. Budget it early.
  2. 2WiFi / internet Navigator app is FREE (schedules, bookings, chat with your group). Real internet ~US$30/device/day, or ~US$49 with streaming; voyage bundles US$88–299 for up to 4 devices. Airplane mode + ship WiFi to dodge roaming. One adult on a bundle is usually enough.
  3. 3Palo Trattoria / Mike & Sulley's Fancy adult dining — best "big night" upgrade. Pre-book, pair with a kids'-club evening.
  4. 4Spa & thermal-suite passes Treatments + steam/lounger sanctuary. Book sea-day mornings.
  5. 5Drink tastings & mixology ~1-hour sessions; first to sell out.
  6. 6Makeovers (BBB / Marvel Style Studio) Big memory-per-dollar if your child's into it; easy skip if not.
  7. 7Alcohol & specialty drinks Cocktails, specialty coffee, bubble tea — all + 18%. Deck soft drinks stay free.
  8. 8Karaoke rooms ~US$135 per room / 1.5 hrs — fun for a group, easy skip otherwise.
  9. 9Photo package Unlimited downloads pre-bookable, or buy singles onboard.
  10. 10Princess tea (Royal Society) Small capacity, keepsakes included.
  11. 11Merchandise Duffy & Friends, Pandora, local-artist series. Set a cap before night one.
  12. 12Nursery Hourly fee, ages 6mo–3yr — your fallback for the 3-year-old if they're not yet toilet-trained (Oceaneer Club requires toilet-trained 3+). Reserve ahead if so.
  • 🧮Rough extras budget (2 adults + 2 kids, 4 nights): gratuities ~US$256 (charged per guest, kids included) · one WiFi bundle ~US$120 · one Palo dinner ~US$100–150 · one makeover ~US$100–250 · drinks/misc US$100+ → realistically US$700–900 on top of the fare, before shopping.
  • 💳The whole bill settles in USD — a low-forex Malaysian card quietly saves a few % across the entire folio.

Ship Rules & Secrets

  • 📏Coaster: 120–195 cm · under-7s ride with someone 14+ · closed-toe shoes with heel strap · no loose items. It's open and running now (the launch-week outage is over), though like any coaster it can pause for weather or maintenance.
  • 🎭Shows need app bookings — theatre seats are reserved, not walk-in. Book at the terminal.
  • 🛍️Shops: shut in port; open ~10:30pm night one (reserved slot); normal at sea; closed the final morning.
  • ⌚No DisneyBand+ here — use your key card + app.
  • 💧No bottled water in cabins — bring refillables; free soft-drink stations on Decks 10 & 17. Slippers not provided.
  • 🕛Booking windows open at midnight US Eastern = 12:00 noon MYT the same day. 4 Aug noon: check-in first, Royal Gathering second.
  • 🍽️Dining rotation is assigned — visible in the app at the terminal; changes at the dining desk on Day 1.
  • 💵Everything bills in USD to your Key to the World card; gratuities auto-charge at the end.
  • 👶Kids' club runs to ~midnight — adults can tour it at open house; the late hours are what free up your evenings.
  • 🔁Cancellations reappear daily — recheck the app each morning for spa/tea/tasting slots.
